# Video production guide

This guide applies to every video of the course. The study sources are the unit manual and the unit practice guide.

## Audience and tone
- University students of Universidad Tecnológica Indoamérica (Ecuador). Address the student directly ("tú" in Spanish, "you" in English), warm and precise, no slang.
- One video explains one idea and ends with what the student will do next in class or in the practice.

## Accuracy rules
- Use only facts, figures, names and examples that appear in the sources. Do not add statistics, dates, authors or quotations from outside.
- When a figure is illustrative in the source, say that it is an example.
- Use the terminology of the manual. Keep technical terms consistent throughout the video.
- Do not read these production notes aloud and do not show them on screen.

## Structure
1. Open with a concrete question or situation from the student's context (the problem the unit addresses).
2. Develop the idea in three or four steps, each with one visual.
3. Close with a one-sentence summary and the task that follows (the practice of the unit).

## Look
- Colours of the university only: deep purple #3C235F, orange #EA600A for highlights, white and light grey.
- No photographs of people, no logos or third-party brands, no decorative clip art.
- Large legible text, one idea per slide, titles written in full.
